07經典 WSB Quotes
WSB 原句與解讀

"bears really wanted to see the world go up in flames just so they could make $50, sad"

這句反映 bulls 目前佔據情緒與道德高地，但當一邊開始把對手描繪成災難受益者，通常代表 positioning 已經偏擠擁。

"Iran directly rejects Trump's new claim... a 'desperate attempt to manipulate the market'"

最重要不是否認本身，而是伊朗也把美方 headline 視為市場工具。未來和平消息的可信度折讓會更高。

"Hilarious that market doesn't give a fuck if the news is real or not it's just autopump on the taco"

這是今次 market microstructure 的核心：短線價格由 keyword、positioning、liquidity 驅動，而非真偽判斷。

"I kept shorting this kind of move because I thought nobody could possibly be this stupid, but eventually you have to accept that it was always only about positioning and liquidity"

這句展示由基本面憤怒轉向 flow-following 的投降過程。當 bears 都承認 liquidity 主導，反而要小心交易過度一致。

"I went from 920,000 to 820,000 in just 6 hours"

關於 MU 暴跌的討論成為 forced liquidation 敘事的證據。它支持 dip-buying 心理，但不等於基本面已重新確認。

"I thought the market was forward-looking and smart, but after watching it take the bait again and again, it's actually dumb as a rock"

這是對有效市場信念的直接崩壞。短線可交易，但若把它當長期 edge，容易高估 meme-driven reflexivity。
