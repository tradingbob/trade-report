07經典 WSB Quotes
WSB 金句｜保留英文原文，中文為交易語境解讀。

"MU, SKHY, and Samsung could all say their capacity is completely sold out until 2030 and the market would still treat them as cyclical that could crash anytime."

這句準確捕捉 memory 板塊困境：即使訂單能見度提升，市場仍用週期股框架定價，beat 不再自動變成催化劑。

"Memory bols got successfully trapped. Now comes the long and slow slide with small relief bounces that incite just enough fake hope to keep people holding."

這不是單純嘲笑，而是典型熊市反彈心理描述：反彈的功能可能是延長持倉痛苦，而非確認反轉。

"My revenue was higher than SMR last quarter. I would like a 3 billion dollar valuation. Thank you."

SMR 被用作高估值概念股的反面教材。社群不是只笑收入低，而是質疑估值與商業驗證完全脫節。

"My memory length is about 24hrs, and my conviction is literally what the guy below me comments."

這句代表 zero conviction 文化。當共識是即時、脆弱而可被留言改寫，社群一致看法反而更有反向指標價值。

"Everyone here was saying shorting SPCX is free money. So inverse time."

SPCX trade 的核心矛盾：基本面供應壓力偏空，但當所有人都把 short 當成 free money，交易本身已經變得危險。
