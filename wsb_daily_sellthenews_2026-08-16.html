WSB 金句與解讀

"The bear case doesn't even require AI to fail. AI could be completely transformative and we could still be massively overbuilding data centers relative to what can actually be monetized."

這是本輪最有價值的風險框架：問題不在 AI 有沒有用，而是 capex 速度是否遠超可變現需求。這個 thesis 可以用 utilization、pricing、lease quality 與 depreciation 去驗證。

"Nvidia now is the (1) seller, (2) investor, (3) customer, (4) cloud residual buyer, and (5) debt guarantor of AI datacenters. Is that bullish? Hard to tell."

這句點出 NVDA 由半導體公司變成 AI capex 金融中樞。市場一旦開始要求 balance-sheet risk premium，估值邏輯會由 revenue growth 轉向 credit quality。

"Micron $1000 August 2026: 'It's not cyclical anymore.' $800 September... $30 December 2026: 'Shut up you dumb bears'"

這不是單純嘲笑，而是直接攻擊 storage bull case 最脆弱假設：每次週期高位都會有人相信這次供需結構永久改變。

"You guys are becoming worthless as indicators... The sentiment on this sub is all over the place. I guess we go higher?"

WSB 自己也意識到 signal 被 chat noise 稀釋。對 sentiment model 而言，這代表 raw bullish comment count 要打折，應提高 position disclosure 與 realized outcome 的權重。
