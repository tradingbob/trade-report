07經典 WSB Quotes
WSB 原句與解讀

"I have taken multiple loans out to buy calls on MU when it's down 5 percent... I'm now in stupid debt. I know I have a problem."

這不是普通 loss post，而是 memory trade 進入 forced deleveraging 的證據。當資金來源由現金變成貸款，訊號價值不在方向，而在倉位壓力。

"My brothers in christ i am well and truly fucked. I gambled away our mortgage money"

語氣似 meme，但內容是家庭資產風險。這類把破產包裝成笑話的留言密集出現，通常代表情緒接近極端。

"SPY -2%, NVDA -13%, GOOG -22%, MSFT -33%, META -26%, TSLA -38%, NFLX -46%"

這句解釋了為何指數看似正常，散戶帳戶卻像 crash。market-cap weighted index 掩蓋了高 beta 與前領漲股的深度回撤。

"stocks'll go up because putting money anywhere else has been made stupid"

這是全晚最完整的 bull case：不是因為 equities 便宜，而是 bonds、gold、cash、BTC 都有缺陷，資金被迫留在股票。

"Weekend reversal off a big red friday is insanely high expectancy to be long"

這代表 TACO trade 已成為共識。若太多人同時押 Monday reversal，真正風險反而是 reversal 不來或先挾後殺。
