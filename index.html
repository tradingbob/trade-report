"Postponing a 'MASSIVE' attack that was never actually going to happen and was so predictable most of WSB expected it physically creates oil and instantly repairs damage to infrastructure and ships. Therefore, Monday oil -8% and SPY +3%."

這句準確諷刺 headline-driven pricing：取消一個市場本來都不完全相信的攻擊，卻被當成同時增加供油、修復基建、推升股市的萬能利好。

"I am a bull but there is no way the war is ending without it escalating more. ... In any case, did you say you wanted the extra napkins in the bag too?"

一個自認好友的人其實講出淡友邏輯，最後用 Wendy’s joke 收尾。重點是判斷與倉位分裂，並非真正 bullish 或 bearish。

"Im afraid Friday was a bear trap. Im also afraid the bounce this week will be a bull trap before the real selloff begins in August. Vol never got bid enough for a sustained pump."

這是樣本中較完整的交易框架：兩邊都有 trap，且用 volatility positioning 解釋為何反彈難以持久。

"It is genuinely impressive how low the bar is for SPY to pump now: 'The Strait is partially open!' → 'At least Iran stopped attacking ships!' → ... → 'A massive attack was postponed!'"

這句點出利好門檻不斷下降。真正要問的是：這些地緣 headline 對 AI monetization、capex return 與 liquidity 有幾大實質影響。

"Average life expectancy USA: 80 years. Average r/wallstreetbets subscriber life expectancy: 34 years."

典型 drawdown 中段幽默。社群仍在用笑話消化虧損，而不是全面止蝕或投降。
