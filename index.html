07經典 WSB Quotes
WSB 金句與市場解讀

"Hertz is so outrageously cheap even if used car liquidation values drop 30% - 40%... That means the P/E would be only 9x."

這句用 HTZ 模仿 MU bull case，精準打擊「低 PE 等於便宜」的懶惰推論。周期股在盈利高峰時低 PE，往往不是 bargain，而是 warning。

"Please talk to an LLM and ask if it's normal for cyclical stocks to have low forward P/Es. Hint: it is. That + initiating share buybacks is typical for cyclical companies at the top of the demand cycle. But this time is of course different."

今批最有資訊量的 bearish comment。它不是情緒宣洩，而是指出 valuation multiple、buyback timing 與需求周期之間的結構性陷阱。

"I know it will crash; I just don’t know when."

這是 WSB 熊軍的核心問題：方向可能有道理，但沒有時間維度就不是交易策略。用短期期權表達長期崩盤 thesis，負 expectancy 很高。

"It starts with a $50 loss, and naturally you want to double down to win it back. Now you’re down $250, and the walls start closing in."

這段不是 meme，而是散戶爆倉心理的真實流程：小虧、加注、失控、all-in。對風控而言，這比任何單一 ticker 更重要。

"AI won’t replace me because I’m already unemployed."

一句話濃縮 AI 焦慮、就業惡化與 K-shaped economy。它解釋了為何社群不相信官方『勞動市場強勁』敘事。
