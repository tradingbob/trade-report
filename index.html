07經典 WSB Quotes
WSB Quotes · 中文解讀

"Nvidia has downsized its planned financial guarantee for an OpenAI data center project in Ohio from $250 billion to less than $120 billion. Monday might be a dump day 📉"

這是全晚資訊含量最高的留言。重點不是一句 bearish call，而是消息在收市後出現，市場未有機會即時定價。

"The market is extremely backward looking. It constantly panics about shit that literally happened weeks ago. Just recently the nasdaq sold off over 10% because the market learned there was a war in Iran 4 months after it started."

這句捕捉到現時地緣風險的延遲定價問題：市場可以長時間無視風險，然後在某個 headline 才一次過補跌。

"13Fs show holdings as of June 30th. He got wrecked in July, so it's not that meaningful."

這是對 KEEL / Leopold copy-trade 最清醒的提醒。13F 是滯後 snapshot，不是即時倉位，也不是買入理由本身。

"I'm doing better than Jane Street, AMA. / I made about 15 billion more than they did in July."

表面是自嘲，實際反映 retail 發現自己持倉同被清盤的大型資金高度重疊，笑話背後是 position crowding 的不安。

"I thought trading was too hard for me, but I spent 12 hours a day, everyday non-stop, backtesting different strategies... and now 5 years later, I realized I was right, trading it's too hard for me."

在一片幻想下週暴賺的氣氛中，這句是 survivorship bias 的反面教材：努力 backtest 不等於有 edge，交易難度沒有因投入時間自動下降。
