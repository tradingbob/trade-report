07經典 WSB Quotes
WSB 原句與解讀

"reddit basically just turned this sub into an aol chatroom lol"

最能概括 live chat 問題：互動變快，但可回溯、可分析的交易資訊密度下降。對 sentiment extraction 是負面變化。

"Bears are 'just waiting for a pullback' to buy in. The 4 month long weakness and consolidation wasn't good enough for them... SPY 490 last year wasn't good enough. It will never be good enough for them."

這不是單純嘲諷淡友，而是指出很多人用等待回調包裝決策癱瘓。當這種說法接近高位時，亦要小心它變成反向情緒訊號。

"Compute is fragmenting. Memory is consolidating."

memory bull case 最乾淨的一句：AI compute 競爭者變多，但記憶體供應仍集中。不過 cyclical margin peak 風險沒有因此消失。

"We're trading Korea, not memory stocks."

這是理解 MU、SNDK 短線表現的核心。估值和供需不是沒用，而是短線輸給 SK hynix、KOSPI 與韓國槓桿倉位。

"I bet there's some regard here that holds through the elevator down but sells halfway through the ladder up"

準確描述散戶在 V 型反彈中的行為缺陷：跌市硬扛，升市過早止賺，最後仍追不到 beta。
