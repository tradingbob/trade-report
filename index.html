"Burry capitulated in LULU... My IV15 fell rather dramatically under new assumptions and probabilities. Negative growth in China, negative growth in the US despite store expansion... Not good at all."

"A crumbling US economy is good for stocks because what matters is central bank intervention is probably a late stage market signal."

這是全日最有質素的自省。當壞經濟數據被機械式解讀成利好流動性，市場可能已進入後周期、敘事壓過基本面的階段。

"Market goes up — semis barely move. Market goes down — semis dump exponentially."

一句講中 WSB 的實際痛點：不是看錯大市，而是持倉集中在落後板塊。這種相對弱勢比指數升跌更影響散戶情緒。

"The market is actively punishing let-winners-run mentality. Take money and get out."

紙手被包裝成紀律，反映高波動升市中參與者更怕回吐多於怕錯過。這可以是趨勢後段的情緒轉折訊號。

"Shorting LULU in my lulu boxers, shorts, and hoodie."

最典型 WSB 式矛盾：承認產品不差，但仍然 short 股票。核心教訓是好公司、好產品、好股票估值，是三件不同事。
