Classic WSB Quotes

"The same sub that told me SPCX was going straight to 50 is also telling me HTZ is going straight to 0."

這句最有交易價值，因為它把社群共識失敗率轉化成反向訊號。meme 股可以在基本面很差時繼續升，原因正是太多人太早認定它必跌。

"HASSETT: IF YOU EXCLUDE ALL THE PEOPLE WHO ARE LOOKING FOR WORK, THE UNEMPLOYMENT RATE IS 0.0%"

這不是單純笑話，而是社群對官方就業敘事失去信任的濃縮版，也解釋了為何壞數據被市場扭成利好。

"Micron: $50 billion in revenue in 2026—‘it’s a cyclical stock’; $150 billion in 2027—‘it’s a cyclical stock’; $1 trillion in 2029—‘shut up, you memory bagholder’"

memory holders 的核心怨氣：他們相信結構性成長，但市場仍用 cyclical multiple 定價。問題是便宜估值本身不會觸發 rerating。

"Doubt it's over for HTZ until we start seeing the ape rocket AI memes appearing everywhere. Y'all remember fake meat."

這是少數可操作的 sentiment exit rule：當 meme 圖密度爆發，代表散戶注意力已到尾段，追入風險高於 squeeze upside。

"No room for berrorists in Nasdaqistan"

典型高位語氣。當 bearish stance 被視為異端，代表市場情緒已很擁擠，不能再把升市本身當作低風險證據。
