"So sandisk cratered to the center of the earth on earnings because 'people are worried about ai' and it has no future, and now coreweave announces they aren't going bankrupt and the stock skyrockets. Alright man."

呢句最準確指出市場敘事不一致：同一個 AI theme，對現金流較實在嘅 storage 公司係利淡，對燒錢 cloud provider 反而係利好。價格主要由 narrative gap 驅動。

"Coreweave record loses and +15%... Rug pull tomorrow? You don't understand, they expected an even higher record loss, so this is bullish."

市場買嘅唔係盈利質素，而係虧損少過預期。呢種 expectation gap trade 可以急升，但一旦宏觀或 guidance 轉差，重估會好快。

"SMCI proves crime is good. Noted. Every time SMCI commits fraud, buy the dip."

治理風險已經被社群當成笑話，短線反而減少阻力；但當風險被 meme 化，代表真正負面消息出現時，價格可能完全無防守。

"CRWV has negative operating margins (!) and a fck ton of interest every quarter (30% of revenue) in addition. You can't just sell your way out of that negative cash flow."

呢個係少數有可驗證指標嘅淡友論點。後續要睇 interest/revenue、operating margin 同 debt growth 有冇改善，否則 backlog 只係延遲風險。

"None of this matters, only CPI does. Go to sleep."

盤後 earnings 狂熱之上，真正風險仍然係明早 8:30 ET CPI。所有 after-hours gain 都隱含一個假設：通脹數據唔會爆雷。
